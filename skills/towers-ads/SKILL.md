# Getting ads ready on Towers

1. Call `run_ads` (with `site` for a company other than their current one). It puts the company's ads mission in its office, from its plan: who sees the ads, their words and the company's address. If the company has an ads mission already, it answers with that one.
2. Nothing runs and nothing is spent yet. Tell them the mission waits for them in the Towers app, and give them its link: it opens the mission with an arrow at its button. There they see who the ads are for, have the ad video made, and choose how many credits to put in.
3. Once the ads run, `numbers` shows the visitors, sign-ups and ad spend they bring, and `next` (with the mission's id) shows how the campaign is doing.
